    Learn to Spot Scams

    Six patterns show up in most scam messages. Once you know them, they're hard to miss.

    Suspicious links

    Check the actual domain before interacting. The part just before .com or .in is what matters.

    sbi.co.in official
    sbi-kyc-verify.xyz not SBI

    Urgency

    Scammers may pressure people into acting quickly, before they have time to check.

    "blocked within 30 minutes"

    Sensitive information

    Never casually share passwords, OTPs, PINs or authentication codes. Banks don't ask for them.

    "share the OTP to verify"

    Too-good-to-be-true offers

    Unexpected rewards or guaranteed financial returns deserve verification, especially if a fee comes first.

    "won ₹25 lakh, pay ₹4,999 to claim"

    Impersonation

    Verify unexpected requests through official channels, even if the name and logo look right.

    "SBI Alerts" <…@gmail.com>

    Unexpected attachments

    Don't open unexpected files or install apps without verifying the sender and context.

    "install AnyDesk so we can help"
